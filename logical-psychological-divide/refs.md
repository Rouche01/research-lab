# References — logical psychological divide

Sources being drawn on. Informal is fine — full citation only if this graduates into a formal paper.

- Richard W. Hamming, *The Art of Doing Science and Engineering: Learning to Learn* (1997; Stripe Press reprint 2020). Origin of the logical vs psychological novelty distinction in this thread — machines produce psychological surprise without logical novelty; Hamming asks whether humans do either, and notes that given the postulates, the rest of mathematics is “merely psychologically novel.”
